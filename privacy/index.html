Privacy Policy: Universal Docs

Last updated: 21 October 2026, app version 1.11.0

Universal Docs is an Android app by Zarfsoft for reading and editing PDFs, documents, spreadsheets and presentations, filling and creating PDF forms, scanning documents and QR codes, recognising English text in scans, writing notes, recording audio, and opening many other common formats such as text, e-books, pictures, archives and emails. This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Your documents never leave your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not request the Internet permission, so it technically cannot send anything over the network.

What the app stores on your device

  • Recent files list: names, sizes and reading positions of files you opened, so you can continue where you stopped.
  • Folders you choose for the libraries: if you tap “Add a folder”, Android remembers that choice for this app. The app keeps a private list of the file names and links it found there, and small cover pictures of e-books, so the library opens quickly. Choose “Stop looking here” to remove a folder.
  • Your signature, initials and name for comments: only if you create them, so they can be placed on documents.
  • Unsaved-change drafts: a temporary copy of an edit in progress, so work is not lost if the app is closed. Drafts are deleted after 30 days.
  • Unfinished scans: pages you scanned but did not finish are kept for 7 days so you can continue, then deleted.
  • Notes: notes you write are kept in the app’s private storage until you delete them.
  • Recordings: audio you record is kept in the app’s private storage until you delete it, and is never uploaded.

All of this stays in the app’s private storage and is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app removes it. “Clear recent files” and “Delete saved signature” are also available inside the app.

Camera

The app asks for the camera only when you tap Scanner or QR Scanner, and explains why first. Camera pictures are analysed on the phone to find the page edges or read the code. They are not uploaded and not kept unless you save or share the scan. QR codes are only read, never opened without your tap, and the app warns about suspicious links. If you say no, everything else in the app still works.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you tap Record in the recorder, and explains why first. Recordings are saved on the phone, can be played back and shared by you, and are never uploaded. Recording continues with the screen off or another app open: a notification shows that it is running and lets you pause, resume or stop it. On Android 13 and later the app also asks to show notifications when you start recording; if you say no, recording still works and you stop it inside the app. If you refuse the microphone, the rest of the app still works.

Text recognition (OCR)

When you export a scan as a Word file or text, English text is recognised on the phone by an engine bundled inside the app. The page image and the recognised text are not sent anywhere. The recognised text is saved only in the file you choose to save or share.

Files and permissions

The app reads only the files you choose, share to it, or find in folders you chose, through Android’s own pickers. It asks for no storage, contacts, location or network permission. It asks for the camera (scanning), the microphone (recording) and notifications (the recording notification), each only when you use that feature. It also declares Android's foreground service permission for the microphone, so a recording you started can continue in the background. When you save, the app writes only to the file or folder you select.

Editing and forms

Text changes, signatures, notes and form fields are written into a copy you save or share. The app never edits the original unless you choose “Replace the original”. Please only edit documents you own or have permission to change.

Data collection and sharing

Universal Docs collects no personal data and shares no data with anyone, including third parties. There are no third-party SDKs; the bundled open-source libraries run locally and have no network access.

How your information is protected and deleted

Everything listed above is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read, and is never sent off the phone. You can delete it at any time inside the app (Clear recent files, delete your signature and initials, delete notes or recordings, Stop looking here for a library folder) or all at once by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Because no information reaches us, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

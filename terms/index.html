The 7-day trial

Universal Docs is a paid app with a trial. For the first 7 days every feature is unlocked, with no sign-in, no account and no card needed. The 7 days start the first time you open the app on your phone. Clearing the app’s data does not start a new trial. Settings shows how many trial days are left. Nothing is charged when the trial ends.

After the trial without Pro

When the trial ends, Universal Docs becomes a document viewer unless you buy Universal Docs Pro. You can still:

  • open, view and search PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, pictures, archives and emails;
  • share the original files;
  • open, play and share the notes, recordings and files you made during the trial.

Editing is not available in any file type, spreadsheet cells included, and the e-book reader is locked.

Universal Docs Pro

Universal Docs Pro is a one-time purchase. It is not a subscription and there is no automatic or repeat charge. Pro unlocks editing, creating documents, mark-up, fill and sign, the PDF tools, conversions, the scanner, the QR tools, notes, the recorder and the e-book reader.

  • Pro belongs to the Google account that bought it. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open Settings and tap Restore purchase.
  • Buying and restoring go through Google Play and need an internet connection at that moment. After that, Pro works offline.
  • If a purchase is refunded or cancelled by Google Play, Pro is removed from the app.

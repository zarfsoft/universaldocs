The 7-day trial

Universal Docs is a paid app with a trial. For the first 7 days every feature is unlocked, with no sign-in, no account and no card needed. The 7 days start when the app is installed on your phone. Clearing the app’s data does not start a new trial, and neither does reinstalling the app while your phone’s Google backup is on. The PRO badge on the home screen and Settings show how many trial days are left. Nothing is charged when the trial ends.

After the trial without Pro

When the trial ends, Universal Docs becomes a document viewer unless you subscribe to Universal Docs Pro. You can still:

  • open, view and search PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, presentations, pictures, archives and emails;
  • share and print the original files;
  • sign PDFs, add initials, dates, ticks and crosses, highlight, draw, comment and fill in PDF forms, then save or share the result;
  • open, play and share the notes, recordings and files you made during the trial.

Other editing is not available in any file type, spreadsheet cells included, and the e-book reader is locked.

Universal Docs Pro

Universal Docs Pro is a yearly subscription. It renews automatically every year, at the price shown, until you cancel. Pro unlocks editing, creating documents, changing PDF text, the PDF tools (such as merge, split, compress, protect and black out), conversions, the QR tools, notes, the recorder, the file manager’s changes and the e-book reader.

  • The app shows the yearly price in your currency, as Google Play gives it, before you subscribe.
  • Cancel anytime in the Google Play Store: your profile picture, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions (or Settings, Manage subscription, in the app). Pro then stays on until the end of the year you have paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • If the yearly price changes, Google Play tells you before it applies, under Google Play’s rules.
  • Pro belongs to the Google account that subscribed. On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open Settings and tap Restore purchase.
  • Subscribing, renewing and restoring are handled by the Google Play Store app, which connects to Google on its own. Universal Docs itself has no internet access. If your phone can’t reach Google Play when a year renews, Pro keeps working for up to 15 more days, and comes back as soon as Google Play confirms the renewal.
  • If a subscription ends, is refunded or is cancelled by Google Play, Pro is removed from the app. Your documents are never touched.
  • Anyone who bought the earlier one-time Universal Docs Pro keeps it.
